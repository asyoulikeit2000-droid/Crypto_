Strategies and final-signal checks

Daily-aligned breakout/pullback requires D1/H4/H1 agreement. H4-led breakout/pullback requires H4/H1 agreement, with D1 as context and BTC H4 not opposing altcoin direction. Both use a closed H1 range break or EMA20 recovery and volume ≥1.2× baseline. H4 targets start at 1.5R; net TP1/stop must be ≥1.2 after modeled costs. Daily targets retain 1R/2R/3R. Both require fresh prices, liquidity, funding, open interest, structural stops and adequate reward/risk.

Context can veto a technical setup: missing required public context, strong conflicting verified net flows, falling network activity, major economic-event blackouts or headline risk. These research rules are unvalidated. Exact USDT entry, stop and TP1/TP2/TP3 prices use Bybit contract tick sizes, with public OKX price corroboration; these are Bybit references. Independently check your OKX or Binance contract price, tick size and fees before entering. An exact fill is not guaranteed. Enter only within the stated window and zone.
